What we detect

Three ways phishing hides. We find all of them.

Submit any suspicious content — our ML models analyse it across three independent modules and combine the results into one risk score.

URL Analyzer

Paste any suspicious link. We extract lexical features — domain age, special characters, keyword patterns — and classify it using a trained Naive Bayes / SVM model.

Cyber Security

Email / Text Analyzer

Paste an email or message. Our NLP pipeline tokenizes and classifies the text for urgency language, impersonation patterns, and social engineering keywords using spaCy and NLTK.

Natural Language Processing

Screenshot Analyzer

Upload a screenshot of a suspicious email or login page. OpenCV extracts logo regions and compares them against known brand templates to detect visual spoofing attempts.

Image Processing
